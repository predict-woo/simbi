Simbi · Concept B Variations

Sound settles into text — seven takes

The chosen direction: waveform on the left, transcript on the right, warm paper and deep teal. Each variation is shown large, then at Dock (64 px) and menu-bar (32 / 16 px) scale on light and dark grounds.

B1

Refined original

The first mockup with tuned spacing and optical centering. The baseline the others deviate from.

B2

Transcript rows

Every line begins as its own little waveform — reads as a diarized transcript, which is literally what Simbi produces.

B3

Inverted

Same mark, teal ground with cream glyph. Heavier presence in the Dock; holds up better against light desktops.

B4

Folded note

The glyph placed on a dog-eared page — leans hardest into "notes app", at the cost of some abstraction.

B5

Tipping bars

One bar rotating through the sequence until it lies down as a line of text — the transformation itself as the mark.

B6

Settling line

The first line of the note is still oscillating; the rest have settled into prose. One continuous stroke, very calm.

B7

Live bar accent

B1 with the tallest bar in warm amber — a quiet nod to the recording indicator, and a second brand color if you want one.
